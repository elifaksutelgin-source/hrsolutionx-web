ABOUT US

A partnership built on leadership experience and focused on your business goals.

HRSolutionX brings more than 20 years of senior HR leadership experience to a boutique consulting approach. We support companies with executive search, HR structuring and organisational transformation.

Our focus is on large businesses, family businesses and international companies in manufacturing, IT and technology, ports and energy. We consider the decisions facing owners, CEOs, boards and HR directors in the context of their business goals and culture.

We begin by understanding your organisation and needs. Together, we define priorities and develop practical solutions for leadership, clear responsibilities and HR processes, supporting the move from recommendations to implementation.
