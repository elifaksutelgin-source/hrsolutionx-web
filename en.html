ABOUT US

Experienced advice. A clear focus on your business.

HRSolutionX is a boutique consultancy drawing on more than 20 years of senior HR leadership experience. We work with businesses on executive search, HR organisation and systems, and organisational transformation.

We work with large enterprises, family-owned businesses and international companies in manufacturing, technology and IT, ports and energy. Our clients include owners, CEOs, boards and HR directors facing important decisions about leadership, people and growth.

We start with your business: its priorities, its culture and the challenges ahead. Together, we define the leadership requirements, organisational responsibilities and HR processes that need attention, then support you in putting the agreed approach into practice.
