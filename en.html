FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our Executive Search process, confidentiality standards and advisory approach.

What is Executive Search and how does it differ from standard recruitment?+

Executive search is a proactive, specialised search methodology designed to identify and attract high-calibre C-Level and board-level executives who are typically not active on job boards. We provide comprehensive market mapping, rigorous competency assessments and confidential direct outreach.

How do you guarantee candidate and client confidentiality (NDA)?+

All executive search and advisory assignments are conducted under strict non-disclosure agreements (NDAs). Candidate identities are fully safeguarded and never disclosed to third parties without prior explicit permission.

How long does an executive search assignment typically take?+

Depending on seniority and scope, initial market mapping and presentation of a calibrated shortlist typically takes 2 to 4 weeks, with final interviews, selection and offer completion concluding within 6 to 8 weeks.

How can senior executives submit their profiles?+

Senior leaders and board directors can securely share their profiles and career aspirations through our Executive Candidates page under complete confidentiality.

Which industries and regions do you cover?+

We serve leading enterprises across Manufacturing, Technology & IT, Energy, Ports & Maritime Logistics, Financial Services and FMCG across Turkey, Europe and the wider region.
