Board and Leadership Advisory

Support sound leadership decisions, clearer responsibilities and continuity at the top.

Navigate leadership transitions

Growth, succession and the transition to professional management can raise difficult questions about leadership and accountability. We work with owners, CEOs and boards to consider those questions in the context of the business and its future needs.

Roles and responsibilities

We help clarify expectations across ownership, the board and executive management. Our work addresses leadership responsibilities, decision-making authority and the working relationships needed to support business objectives.

Professional management in family businesses

The transition involves more than recruiting an executive. We help owners and leaders work through expectations, responsibilities, authority and culture, supporting a productive relationship between the family and professional management.

Succession and continuity

We help assess the future requirements of critical leadership roles. Depending on the brief, we consider succession options, potential transitions and development needs.

Agree the advisory brief

We begin with your leadership agenda, current governance structure and the decisions ahead. Together, we agree the scope, participants and expected outputs.
