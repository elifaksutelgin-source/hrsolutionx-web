Executive Search

We help you define the leadership your business needs and make an informed executive appointment.

When can we help?

When filling a critical leadership role, building a team for a new investment or moving towards professional management, a candidate’s experience needs to be considered alongside the business context. We approach executive search through your objectives, the responsibilities of the role and your company culture.

Defining success in the role

We work with the company owner, CEO, board or HR director to clarify expectations. Together, we define the responsibilities, decision-making authority, experience and leadership capabilities the role requires.

Candidate research and assessment

We research candidates against the agreed profile and assess their experience, leadership approach and fit with the role. Our findings support your company’s selection decisions.

Agreeing the scope

The role profile, candidate research, assessment and support during selection are agreed at the start. Timing and milestones depend on the position and your business needs.

Our focus

We focus on leadership needs in manufacturing, IT and technology, ports and energy, working towards the needs of large businesses, family businesses and international companies. The first discussion clarifies your priorities and the scope of the search.
