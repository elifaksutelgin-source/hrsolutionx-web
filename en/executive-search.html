Executive Search

Appoint leaders with the experience, judgement and leadership approach your business needs.

Leadership appointments that matter

A new investment, a period of growth or a change at the top can make the next leadership appointment particularly important. We look beyond a candidate’s track record to understand what the role requires, where the business is heading and how the leadership team works.

Define the brief

We work with owners, CEOs, boards and HR directors to establish a clear search brief. This sets out the mandate, responsibilities, decision-making authority and experience needed, alongside the leadership qualities that will matter in your organisation.

Identify and assess candidates

We research candidates against the agreed brief and assess their experience, leadership approach and suitability for the role. Our findings give you a clearer basis for comparing candidates and making your appointment.

Support the selection process

At the outset, we agree the scope of research, assessment and support through the selection process. Timelines and milestones reflect the role, the market and your business priorities.

Start with the business context

Our focus spans manufacturing, technology and IT, ports and energy. We work with large enterprises, family-owned businesses and international companies. An initial conversation helps us understand your priorities and define the search.
