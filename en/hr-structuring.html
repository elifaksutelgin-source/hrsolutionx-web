HR Organisation and Systems

Build the HR structure and core processes your business needs to grow.

A stronger foundation for HR

Whether you are establishing an HR function, scaling a growing business or improving existing systems, the starting point is the same: a clear understanding of your organisation, people and business priorities.

Organisation design and accountability

We help define roles, reporting lines and responsibilities so that your structure supports the business and people understand what is expected of them.

Core people processes

Depending on your priorities, our work can cover recruitment, onboarding, performance management and employee development. We design processes around your resources, management approach and capacity to put them into practice.

A practical implementation plan

We sequence the work around the areas that need attention first. Together, we agree the process design, ownership and implementation steps, with clear points for reviewing progress.

Where to begin

We discuss your current HR function, growth plans and operational challenges. This helps determine what to address first and how to organise the work.
