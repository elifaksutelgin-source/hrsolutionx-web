HR Structuring

We help you build an HR structure that fits your company’s scale, growth objectives and day-to-day operations.

When can we help?

We focus on companies establishing HR for the first time, growing quickly or improving existing processes. We start by assessing your current structure, team needs and business priorities together.

Organisation and responsibilities

We work on organisation design, role descriptions, reporting relationships and areas of responsibility. The aim is a structure that supports business goals and makes individual roles clearer.

Recruitment and core HR processes

Depending on the scope, we address recruitment, onboarding, performance and employee development. Processes are designed around your resources, management approach and capacity to implement them.

Priorities and implementation

We agree priorities rather than introducing every system at once. Process design, responsibilities and implementation steps are planned within the agreed scope, with progress reviewed at defined decision points.

The first discussion

We explore your current HR structure, growth agenda and the areas where support is most needed. This helps determine which processes to address first and how the work will be organised.
