Talent and Performance Management

Connect business priorities with clear expectations, meaningful feedback and employee development.

Make performance management useful

Unclear goals, inconsistent assessments and informal development planning can leave a gap between business priorities and everyday work. We help you review the current approach and identify where a clearer framework would make a difference.

Goals and assessment

We translate business priorities into relevant objectives for teams and individuals. Goals and assessment criteria should be clear, measurable where appropriate and suited to the way your business operates.

Feedback and development

Effective performance management is an ongoing conversation. We support managers in giving feedback, discussing development and turning assessment into practical next steps.

Talent and career planning

Within the agreed scope, we help identify critical roles, talent needs and development priorities. Career paths and learning plans are considered in light of the capabilities your business will need in the future.

Define the scope

Together, we decide which elements of goal-setting, assessment, feedback, talent management and career planning to address. We then agree responsibilities, implementation steps and progress reviews.
