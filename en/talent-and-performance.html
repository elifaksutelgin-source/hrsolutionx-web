Talent and Performance Management

We help you connect business goals with employee contribution through a talent and performance approach that supports development.

When can we help?

Unclear expectations, inconsistent assessment criteria or an informal approach to development can create a gap between business objectives and everyday work. We review your existing approach and the areas you want to improve.

Goals and assessment criteria

We connect business goals with team and employee responsibilities. We work on relevant, understandable and trackable objectives and assessment criteria that fit your company’s scale and working practices.

Feedback and development

Performance management is more than a year-end rating. We work on managers’ feedback approach, development conversations and employee support, connecting assessment with growth.

Critical talent and career planning

Depending on the scope, we help identify critical roles, talent needs and development priorities. Career paths and learning priorities are considered alongside the company’s future requirements.

Agreeing the scope

We clarify which goal-setting, assessment, feedback, talent and career processes to address. Implementation steps, responsibilities and progress reviews are agreed together.
