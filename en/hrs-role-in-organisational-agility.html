HR’s Role in Organisational Agility

An organisation’s ability to respond to changing markets depends on more than technology. People need to understand priorities, make decisions at the right level and work effectively across teams. HR has an important role in creating the conditions for that to happen.

From process administration to organisational change

Alongside essential day-to-day processes, HR can help shape how the organisation works. Clear roles, simpler coordination and regular performance conversations give managers and employees a better basis for responding to changing priorities.

Make feedback timely and useful

An annual review alone rarely provides all the direction people need. Regular feedback helps managers clarify expectations, address issues earlier and connect development with current business needs. The approach should be practical enough to become part of everyday management.

Culture and employee commitment

Change can create uncertainty. People are more likely to engage when they understand the purpose, can raise concerns and see how their work contributes. HR can support leaders in communicating clearly and building a culture in which employees take an active part in change.
