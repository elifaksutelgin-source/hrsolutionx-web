Executive Candidates

HRSolutionX BLACK gives senior executives a way to share their experience and career interests with us. We consider profiles in relation to the leadership searches we undertake.

  1. Share your profile

    Tell us about your experience and the leadership roles that interest you.

  2. Profile assessment

    We review your experience against the requirements of relevant executive searches.

  3. Stay in consideration

    If a suitable opportunity arises, we may contact you to discuss it. Submitting a profile does not guarantee an interview or appointment.
