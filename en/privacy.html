Privacy notice

Last updated: October 2026. This page explains the website’s candidate and enquiry data flow in English. The Turkish privacy notice remains available.

Information collected

When you submit a candidate profile or enquiry, we receive the information you provide, such as your name, email address, company, career summary and optional phone number or LinkedIn profile.

Purpose

Information is used for executive candidate assessment, communication about relevant opportunities, and responding to corporate enquiries.

Profile sharing

Your CV and contact details are shared with potential employers or partner organisations only after your approval, as stated in our Turkish privacy notice. This is distinct from the technical processing needed to transmit forms.

Form transmission

Website forms use Formspree. Information entered in a form is processed and transmitted through that service. Refer to the provider’s privacy policy for its processing and transfer practices.

Updates and requests

Contact info@hrsolutionx.com to request an update, deletion or information about the handling of your data. Please do not submit identity documents, health information or other sensitive personal information through these forms.
