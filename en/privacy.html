Privacy Policy and Personal Data Notice

Last updated: October 2026

HRSolutionX Consulting (the “Company”, “we” or “us”), acting as data controller, is committed to the processing and protection of personal data relating to candidates, clients and website visitors in accordance with Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (“KVKK”) and applicable legislation.

1. Personal data we collect

When you share a candidate profile or contact us through the website, we collect the information you provide. This may include your name, email address, telephone number, CV information such as education, employment history and skills, and a link to your LinkedIn profile.

2. Why we process personal data

We process personal data to carry out executive searches, maintain our talent pool so that we can consider candidates for relevant career opportunities, manage communication with candidates and clients, improve our services and meet legal obligations.

3. Sharing personal data

We share your CV and contact details with potential employers or partner organisations only after obtaining your explicit consent or approval. Your data is not sold or transferred to third parties for commercial purposes, except where disclosure is required by law.

Website form processing

The approval requirement above concerns sharing your profile with potential employers. Website forms are processed and transmitted through Formspree. Information you enter into a form passes through that service. You can review the provider’s processing and transfer practices in the Formspree Privacy Policy.

4. Your information

Please share personal information only where it is relevant to your application or enquiry. Requests to update, share or delete your information can be sent to info@hrsolutionx.com.

5. Your rights and how to contact us

To exercise your rights under Article 11 of the KVKK, including requests to delete or update your personal data or find out who it has been shared with, please contact info@hrsolutionx.com.
